The studio

HHot Designs started as a fabric swatch on a kitchen table and became a studio that isn't afraid of color.

We're a Cairo-born, globally-minded design studio for people who want their homes to feel considered, not curated into blandness. Every project starts with how a room is actually used — then we layer in the texture, tone, and a little bit of drama.
